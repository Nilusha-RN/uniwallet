About UniWallet

UniWallet is a free, student-first expense management app built to help undergraduates at Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka track daily spending, set budgets, and understand their financial habits.All stored privately in the cloud.

This project was developed as part of an academic initiative to solve the real challenge faced by university students managing limited monthly allowances.

How It Works

1
Sign in with Google

Your data is tied to your Google account — private and secure, only you can see it.

2
Set Your Balance

Enter your current wallet or account balance on the Overview page to get an accurate picture.

3
Log Transactions

Add income or expenses with a category, note, and date. All entries are saved instantly to the cloud.

4
Analyse & Budget

View weekly charts, category breakdowns, and set monthly budget limits with live progress tracking.

5
Export to Excel

Download a detailed Excel report of any month — transactions, weekly breakdown, and category summary.

Install as a Mobile App

Free • No App Store needed

UniWallet works as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can add it to your phone's home screen and use it like a native app-no downloads required.

Android (Chrome)
  1. Open UniWallet in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right)
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen"
  4. Tap "Add" — done!
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open UniWallet in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" — done!
Once installed, UniWallet opens full-screen just like a native app, with no browser bar.
